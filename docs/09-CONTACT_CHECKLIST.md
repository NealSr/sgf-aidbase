## Gold Quotes to Listen For

If ANY of these come up in your calls/emails, write them down VERBATIM for your demo:

- "People call us who should have called someone else"
- "The hardest part is people not knowing we exist"
- "We get calls every day from people who don't know where to start"
- "I spend half my time just connecting people to the right place"
- Anything about how 211 doesn't work well or is hard to navigate
- Anything about language barriers or accessibility

One real quote from a real org in your demo is worth more than 10 extra features. Get that quote.
